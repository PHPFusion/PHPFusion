What is a Credit Verification Value/Card Verification Code (CVV/CVC)?

The Card Verification Code (CVC) is a 3-digit number found on the signature panel on the back of your credit card. It is an additional safeguard that helps us validate your purchase and protect against fraud. It is not contained in the magnetic stripe information and is therefore not printed on sales receipts.

Where is the CVV/CVC located?

Visa's Card Verification Value (CVV) is a three-digit number on the back of your card. The full credit card number is reprinted in the signature box and at the end of the number is the CVV.

Validation Code (CVC) is a three-digit number on the back of your card. The full credit card number is reprinted in the signature box and at the end of the number is the CVC.

Card Identification Digits (CID) refers to the 4-digit number on the front of the Card above the Card number. You have been requested to provide this information for American Express to validate for security reasons.
