Polaris Theme
===

**Polaris: A Guiding Light**  

Polaris, the North Star, has long served as a beacon for travelers seeking direction in the night sky. A constant presence in the northern hemisphere, it has guided countless journeys home. In that same spirit, may this project serve as a guiding light—bringing clarity, direction, and inspiration to all who embark on this journey with us.  

I’m excited to announce a new theme for **PHPFusion.com**! Designed with a clean and modern aesthetic, this theme prioritizes usability and accessibility, ensuring a seamless experience for all users.  

### Key Features:  
✅ Customizable colors, fonts, and images  
✅ Sleek, modern design with a focus on accessibility  
✅ Optimized for performance and user experience  

To install the theme, simply **download the zip file** from the **GitHub repository** and extract it into your PHPFusion installation directory. Then, activate it by navigating to the **"Themes"** section in the admin panel and selecting the new theme from the dropdown menu.  

I look forward to your feedback and hope you enjoy using the theme as much as I enjoyed creating it! Feel free to reach out with any questions or suggestions.
